Business · Solution

How it works

1

The store prices it

It issues a rug certificate at its own buying price, $600, and keeps the rug safe.

2

An investor lends it

USDC

$600 in USDC for 1 to 12 months. The weaver gets $540 at once.

3

Repay, or store buys

The weaver repays $600 and takes the rug home. If not, the store must buy it at its price within 7 days.

One rug, 6 months

Investor lends$600
Interest 15% a year−$45
Platform fee 1%, once−$6
Store fee 0.25% a month−$9
Weaver gets now$540

Why it’s different

The store’s own buying price is the collateral value. It can’t overprice: it may have to buy at that price. It can’t lowball much: the weaver can go to another store.

  • Sell todayThe $900 gap goes to the store.
  • Informal loanCan’t repay? The weaver still owes.
  • RugDealerShe keeps a later sale’s upside, and owes nothing more if the store buys.

How it ends · same $600, 6-month loan

Weaver repays

Weaver pays $600, rug back: cost $60

Investor gets $645 · Store earned $9

Store buys

Weaver keeps $540, owes nothing

Store pays $600, owns the rug · Investor gets $645

Store misses buy-out

Store is frozen: no new certificates

Investor takes rug + $45 · Weaver owes nothing

Business model

Stores join: buy-outs happen at their normal price, and they earn a fee for space they already have.

Network fees are under $0.10 a loan, so a small loan pays its own way at market prices. No subsidy needed.
Technology

Built on Stellar

  • One Soroban contract holds rug, loan and USDC, and enforces the buy-out.
  • ~5 s settlement, sub-cent fees
  • USDC + anchor to lira (mocked)

On-chain

  • Rug certificates
  • Loans + limits
  • USDC + windows
  • Events

Off-chain

  • Rug at the store
  • Store agreement
  • Anchor (mocked)
  • Next.js on RPC

Security & trust

  • No borrowing on your own rug, no funding your own loan
  • Rug hand-back: weaver + store sign
  • Stuck payouts held: every loan closes
  • Missed buy-out: store frozen
  • Platform can’t move money or rugs

UX: the weaver’s terms

Fees shown in lira before signing. One desk per role, a plain-sentence feed, and no wallet needed to look around.
