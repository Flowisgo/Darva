# Narration: RugDealer, 60 seconds

Read this over the silent master during the pitch, or record it to make the narrated master (see [README](README.md#narrated-master)). About 150 words a minute, dry, no music. Each line fits its slot with room to breathe: `npm run narration:check` times every line with a synthetic voice and fails if one runs long.

Say "Ayşe" as **EYE-sheh**.

| Time | Say | Cue |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–5.6 | A buyer pays fifteen hundred for Ayşe's rug. The store offers six hundred. | Start as the video starts. |
| 5.6–8.0 | So she borrows that price instead. | Scroll to the three outcomes. |
| 8.0–16.9 | The store writes the rug's certificate at its own buying price, signed in a real wallet, for a fraction of a cent. | Pause on "signed": the Varial window opens. |
| 16.9–25.3 | Ayşe borrows that price for six months. Fees come off once: she gets five hundred forty today. | "Once" lands as the fee breakdown is ringed. |
| 25.3–31.6 | Deniz funds the whole loan in one transaction, and gets six forty-five back. | "One transaction" on the Lend click. |
| 31.6–44.1 | A buyer turns up, so she repays: sixty dollars in fees, not nine hundred lost. She signs to collect, the store co-signs, and the rug goes home. | Breathe after "lost": the collect signature starts. |
| 44.1–51.5 | If she can't repay, the store must buy the rug at the price it set. So it can't lowball her. | Hold "price it set" on the Buy click. |
| 51.5–59.6 | Every step is a Stellar transaction you can check. Borrow against your rug; don't sell it cheap. | Last sentence over the end card. |

About 120 words.
